# Concept exploration prompt

Built-in image generation was used only for the first, three-concept exploration board. The selected direction was then reconstructed as exact SVG geometry and used to render the production boards.

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: app icon concept exploration board for a premium iPad piano-practice app
Primary request: show exactly three distinct, flat, geometric app-icon concepts in a single square presentation board, each as an unmasked 1024x1024-style square with no baked rounded corners and no text. Concept A: a precisely drawn black fermata whose arc also suggests the raised lid of a concert grand, on warm cream paper. Concept B: a pair of adjacent piano keys reduced to architectural black-and-cream geometry, with one small circular notehead counterform. Concept C: a bold black grand-staff brace enclosing a single cream notehead, simplified to remain legible at 29 px. Make all three feel like modern Urtext music engraving, serious, adult, warm, quietly confident, suitable beside native Apple apps. Use exact-looking flat palette only: cream #FBF8F1, ink #161513, nocturne #0C0E12; no amber. Show each concept large and as a tiny 29 px test below it.
Scene/backdrop: clean Nocturne presentation board, dark #0C0E12 with restrained cream labels A, B, C only
Style/medium: crisp vector-logo presentation, flat solid fills, print-sharp edges, no texture, no shadows, no gradients
Composition/framing: three equal columns with generous margins and strict alignment
Constraints: exactly three icon concepts; no words beyond single-letter labels A B C; no extra decorative elements; no text inside icons; strong silhouette; balanced negative space; no rounded-corner masks; no watermark
Avoid: treble clef, flying notes, soundwaves, neon, arcade style, photoreal pianos, piano-key swooshes, vintage flourishes, paper texture, gradients, mockup devices
```
