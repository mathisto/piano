Margins & grid

7% horizontal margin. Header sits at 7% from top; title block uses 24%; incipit receives the remaining 52–57%.

Grand / single staff

Grand staff is default. Solo line gets 1.45× staff size and vertical centering. Voice + piano keeps three staves at 600; card shows voice plus reduced grand staff.

Long & dense

Title: two lines maximum, then collection moves to metadata. Fit fewer complete beats before shrinking. Minimum staff space: 8 pt large, 5 pt card.

Colour & difficulty

No semantic colour. Five 1 pt ink segments encode level; active segments use 68% ink, inactive 18%. This preserves amber and blue meanings.

Crop only at a barline or beat boundaryNever horizontally squash notation
