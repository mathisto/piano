Salamander Grand Piano V3, recorded and edited by Alexander Holm.

Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3 (48 kHz 24-bit, 16 velocity layers; FLAC from https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano)
These 90 MP3 files are derived from it by scripts/make-piano.py: three velocity layers (soft, medium, hard) for each of the 30 recorded notes (every third semitone, A0 to C8), mixed to mono, trimmed, faded and re-encoded at 96 kbps.
