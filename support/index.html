Support

Tell me what got in the way. You will reach the person who builds Piano Forte, not a ticket maze.

Write to me

pianoforte@mattkelly.io

Email opens only when you choose it. Piano Forte does not send your practice history or files.

What to include

  • Whether you are using the iPad app or the browser edition.
  • What you expected, what happened instead and the last thing you tapped.
  • Your iPadOS version, or your browser and computer, when it may matter.

For a library-score problem, use Report a problem from that piece. The email draft includes its title, source, bar and Piano Forte release identifier. Review it before sending.

Before reinstalling

Open Settings → Your data → Backup → Export. Deleting Piano Forte removes the practice history, settings and music stored only inside that installation. A backup or your chosen sync folder is the recovery path.

What happens next

I will use your message only to answer you and investigate the problem. Please do not send a score or backup unless I ask and you have the right to share it.
