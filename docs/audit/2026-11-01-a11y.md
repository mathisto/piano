# Piano Forte accessibility audit (WCAG 2.2 AA, VoiceOver, keyboard, switch)

Date: 2026-11-01. Commit audited: `2b653ae` (main, clean tree; no repo file was edited).

## How this was tested
- **Browser (verified):** Vite dev server on :5403. Headless Chrome via playwright-core 1.64, with axe-core 4.14 using the tags `wcag2a/2aa/21a/21aa/22aa` and best-practice, for violations and incompletes. Viewports 1180×820 and 820×1180, in both dark (Nocturne) and light (Matinée) via `colorScheme`. Touch emulation (`hasTouch`; `pointer: coarse` matched and `hover: hover` did not). Reduced motion via `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`. Browser zoom of 200% and 400% emulated as 590×410 and 320×256 CSS viewports at DPR 2. WCAG 1.4.12 text-spacing injection. Scripts and logs are in `scratchpad/audit/*.mjs` and `*.log`; screenshots are in `scratchpad/audit/a11y/`.
- **Screens covered by axe** (the JSON is `scratchpad/audit/axe-{dark,light}-1180x820.json` and `axe-dark-820x1180.json`):
  - all seven pages;
  - the Settings drawer, and the Help, Credits and Set up sheets;
  - the keyboard chip panel and a song row's ⋯ menu;
  - the start card, the Aids popover, the count-in, a run playing and a paused run;
  - Perform results, the drill (ready, running, results), the Sight Run builder (both paces), a Theory card (before and after an answer), the Scales quiz and the look-through card.
  - Not covered: the Options sheet, which needs an imported file. Its axe coverage is in e2e feature 22.
- **Not run:** real VoiceOver, Switch Control, Voice Control, Full Keyboard Access, or WKWebView on a device. Every VoiceOver or iOS statement below is **inferred from code** and marked so.

## Summary
| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| P0 | 0 |
| P1 | 8 |
| P2 | 9 |
| P3 | 8 |

axe found **no serious or critical violations on any screen in either mode**. Its only violations were `heading-order` (the drawer) and `scrollable-region-focusable` (the drill's ready card); both are P3 below. Touch targets all reach 44 px under a coarse pointer. The focus ring is visible on every control in both modes. The real problems are behavioural, and axe cannot see them: Escape handling, a scrubber that will not move, feedback that vanishes under Reduce Motion, status messages that are re-created rather than updated, and text that cannot scale in the iPad app.

Accessibility health (impeccable rubric): **2/4**. The ARIA effort is good, and semantics and targets are strong, but several verified AA failures sit in the core practice loop.

## P1: WCAG AA failures, or significant difficulty

### A11Y-1 · Escape on the Tempo or Practice button leaves the piece and drops focus to `<body>`
- **Severity:** P1. **WCAG:** 2.4.3 Focus Order and 3.2.2 On Input (unexpected change of context); the APG menu-button pattern. **Status:** **verified in browser.** **Call:** clear.
- **Surface:** play screen, the dock pop-ups (`#tempoBtn`, `#aidsBtn`), before a run (ready) and after one (results).
- **Where:**
  - `src/dock.ts:146-151`: the Escape listener sits on the *menu*, so it only fires when focus is inside the menu.
  - `src/app/input.ts:56-59`: the page's Escape calls `leave()`.
  - Opening a pop-up leaves focus on its button, which is outside the menu. The same applies on iPad, where a tap gives no focus at all.
- **Evidence:** `kbd-dark.log` and `probe6`. Open Practice, press Escape: `play on? false`, focus `BODY`. Same for Tempo: `Escape on tempo button -> { play: false, focus: 'BODY' }`. Escape with focus inside the menu works (`menu: false, focus: tempoBtn`).
- **Fix:**
  - Listen on the `.pop` wrapper rather than the `.popmenu`: `p.addEventListener('keydown', …)` covers both the button and the menu.
  - Or, in `input.ts`, close any open dock pop-up first: `if (dock.closeMenus?.()) return` (have `closeMenus` return whether one was open).
  - Do the same for `#dev` / `#kbMenu`. The shared handler in `src/main.ts:115-120` has the same "inside the menu only" condition; it gets away with it today only because a click moves focus to the first menu item.

### A11Y-2 · The Learn scrubber cannot be moved past bar 1 by keyboard, and an A-B loop can only be dragged
- **Severity:** P1. **WCAG:** 2.1.1 Keyboard (A) and 2.5.7 Dragging Movements (AA). **Status:** **verified in browser.** **Call:** clear.
- **Surface:** dock `#scrub` (`role=slider`, Learn).
- **Where:** `src/dock.ts:334-340`.
  - ArrowRight jumps to `bars[i].t0 - 2 beats` of pre-roll.
  - The next keypress recomputes `cur` from `game.t`, which is now back inside the previous bar, so the position never advances.
  - Home, End, PageUp and PageDown are not handled.
  - A custom A-B loop exists only as a drag (`pointerup` with more than 6 px of travel, lines 320-332).
- **Evidence:** `probe6`: `scrub after 2x ArrowRight ['1', null, …]` and `scrub End ['1', …]`. The slider also has no `aria-valuemin` and no `aria-valuetext`.
- **Fix:**
  - Keep a `this.kbBar` index that the arrows step instead of deriving it from `game.t`.
  - Add Home and End.
  - Set `aria-valuemin="1"` and `aria-valuetext="Bar n of m"`.
  - For loops, a tap-tap alternative: "Set A" and "Set B" items in the Practice menu, or Shift+Arrow to extend a loop from the current bar.

### A11Y-3 · Under Reduce Motion, the Sheet-view grade word and the hit colour vanish, so a Perfect hit gives no feedback at all
- **Severity:** P1. **WCAG:** none strictly (2.3.3 is AAA), but it fails PRODUCT.md's own Accessibility commitment and the impeccable motion rule ("a global 0.01 ms kill that destroys useful feedback"). The severity is P1 because Reduce Motion users lose the core judgement. **Status:** **verified in browser.** **Call:** clear.
- **Surface:** play screen, Staff view.
- **Where:**
  - `src/style.css:2198-2205` is the global `animation-duration: 0.01ms`.
  - `#staff .gr` runs `gradeword` (`style.css:1370, 1382`), and `staff.ts:529` removes the word on `animationend`, so it is gone within 0.01 ms.
  - `ignite` (`style.css:1256`) ends on Paper Played at once.
  - Great, Good and Late keep their small `gshape`; Perfect has no shape, by design.
- **Evidence:** `motion.mjs`.
  - With no preference, 250 ms after a note: `words:[Perfect@1]`, head fill `rgb(19,121,75)`.
  - Under reduce: `words:[]`, head fill `rgb(120,115,106)` (played) at 80, 250 and 500 ms.
- **Fix:** give Reduce Motion a non-moving alternative instead of the kill.
  - In the reduce block: `#staff .gr { animation: gradeword-still 1s steps(1) forwards }`, with `@keyframes gradeword-still { 0%,99% {opacity:1} 100% {opacity:0} }`, and exclude `#staff .gr, #staff [class*='hit-']` from the global rule with `:not()`.
  - Or hold `ignite`'s grade colour with `steps()`.
  - Also make `reduced` (`src/app/state.ts:18`) a live `MediaQueryList` rather than a boot-time boolean.

### A11Y-4 · Theory and Quiz feedback lives in a `role=status` that is re-created on each render, and focus drops to `<body>`
- **Severity:** P1, and potentially P0 for a blind user with a MIDI keyboard. The ear trainers are otherwise a natural fit for them. **WCAG:** 4.1.3 Status Messages (AA) and 2.4.3. **Status:** DOM replacement and focus loss are **verified**; VoiceOver's silence is **inferred** (not run on a device). **Call:** clear.
- **Surface:** Theory cards (all trainers, including ear and rhythm), the Scales quiz and the Memorise live line.
- **Where:**
  - `src/app/theory-ui.ts:30` re-renders `#theory` whole through `theoryView`.
  - The verdict is the `<p class="line" role="status">` at `src/ui-views.ts:409`; the quiz equivalent is at `src/ui-views.ts:339`.
  - A live region inserted together with its text is announced unreliably, and iOS VoiceOver usually skips it.
- **Evidence:** `probe5`. After an answer, `same: false` (the status node was replaced), MutationObserver `childList:theory +2 -2`, `focus: BODY#`. `probe4`: clicking Start leaves focus on `<body>`.
- **Fix:**
  - Keep one persistent `role=status` per page and write only its `textContent`. The existing `say()` (`src/app/state.ts:116`) already does this with `#live`, so call `say('Not quite. F A♭ C.')` on each answer.
  - Wrap the re-render in the existing `keepFocus()` (`src/ui.ts:20`).
  - After an answer, move focus to `#thNext`, or to the card heading when a new card is dealt.

### A11Y-5 · Single-character shortcuts cannot be turned off or remapped
- **Severity:** P1. **WCAG:** 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts (A). **Status:** verified by code read and by the tab-walk run. **Call:** judgement call (the computer-key piano is a deliberate input).
- **Where:**
  - `src/app/input.ts:51-72` handles `?`, `m`, `n`, `b`, `v`, `-`, `=`, `+`, `[` and `]`. `n` toggles note names globally, on any page, and also fires while a *button* has focus.
  - `src/midi.ts:91-107` is the computer-key piano: `a` to `;`, `w e t y u o p`, and `z`/`x`.
  - None has an off switch. Voice Control and speech users can trigger them by accident.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a Settings → Advanced switch, "Single-key shortcuts", default on, that gates both listeners.
  - Or make `m`, `n`, `v`, `b` and `?` active only on the play screen, and leave the piano keys active only while a piece, drill or card is open (`current || theoryOpen() || quizOpen()`).
  - The iPad menu bar already offers ⌘ forms (`AppDelegate.swift:18-44`), so those would remain.

### A11Y-6 · Nocturne control borders on Panel Hover are 2.89:1, below 3:1
- **Severity:** P1. **WCAG:** 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA). **Status:** **verified in browser.** **Call:** clear.
- **Surface:**
  - every bordered control inside a Settings group (`.sgroup` is Panel Hover `#222731`): the Delay field, Calibrate, the segmented controls and selects;
  - any other control placed on `--panel-3`.
- **Where:** `src/style.css:28`, `--edge: light-dark(#7d776b, #666d7d)`. `src/palette.test.ts` checks Edge only on `panel` in Nocturne, not on `panel-2` or `panel-3`.
- **Evidence:** `nontext.mjs`.
  - `drawer number field / Calibrate / seg outer / select: 2.89:1 (rgb(102,109,125) on rgb(34,39,49))`.
  - Nocturne Edge elsewhere is 3.18-3.72:1; Matinée is 3.51-4.45:1.
- **Fix:** lighten the dark Edge to about `#727a8b`, which gives ≥3.3:1 on panel-3 and ≥4:1 on panel. Add `edge` against `panel-2` and `panel-3` to the Nocturne cases in `palette.test.ts`.

### A11Y-7 · The iPad app cannot scale text: px type, no Dynamic Type, probably no zoom
- **Severity:** P1. **WCAG:** 1.4.4 Resize Text (AA), read through WCAG2ICT for a native wrapper. **Status:** **inferred from code**; verify pinch-zoom on the device. **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:**
  - `src/style.css` sets 114 font sizes in px and 2 in em/rem; the body is `font: 15px/1.45` (`style.css:106`).
  - Nothing reads `-apple-system-body` or `UIContentSizeCategory`.
  - `capacitor.config.json` sets `"scrollEnabled": false`, and WKWebView has no browser zoom UI.
  - In a browser, 200% zoom works for pages (see A11Y-12 for the play screen).
- **Fix:**
  - Read iOS's text size through `font: -apple-system-body` on `:root`. WKWebView scales this with Dynamic Type, so derive a `--ts` ratio from the computed size of a probe element.
  - Or have `PianoNative.swift` post `UIApplication.preferredContentSizeCategory` and set a root scale.
  - Then move the shell's type to `rem`. The staff and the canvas can keep their own zoom (`-`/`+`).
  - Verify on the iPad that pinch-zoom works in WKWebView with `scrollEnabled: false`.

### A11Y-8 · "Waiting for you" is re-announced on every note in Learn and twice per drill card
- **Severity:** P1. It is not a strict WCAG failure, but it constantly interrupts a screen-reader user who is playing, and the brief asked about chattiness. **WCAG:** 4.1.3 (spirit) and 2.2.4 Interruptions (AAA). **Status:** the mutations are **verified**; the speech is **inferred**. **Call:** clear.
- **Where:** `src/app/play.ts:284-285` runs `say('Waiting for you')` on every phase change. `src/app/state.ts:116-118` replaces the text node each time, and a replaced node is re-announced by VoiceOver and NVDA.
- **Evidence:** `live.mjs`. Learn: 12 notes gave 12 announcements. Drill: 10 cards gave 20, all "Waiting for you".
- **Fix:** only say it on the first wait of a run, or when entering a wait after a pause. Guard `say()` with `if (msg === last && Date.now() - lastAt < 10000) return`.

## P2: significant barriers, not strict failures

### A11Y-9 · Leaving a piece or results with Escape drops focus to `<body>`
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 2.4.3. **Status:** **verified.** **Call:** clear.
- **Where:** `src/app/play.ts:133-149`. `leave()` calls `showSection(to)` and never restores focus.
- **Evidence:** `kbd-dark.log`: `after Escape on results: section library {"id":"BODY"}`. Leaving through the brand button keeps focus on that button, which is fine.
- **Fix:** remember the opener (the row's Learn or Start button, or the Today row's action) when a piece opens, and focus it, or the section's `h1` with `tabindex=-1`, after `showSection`.

### A11Y-10 · `role="menu"` without menu keyboard behaviour
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 4.1.2 and 1.3.1 (the role promises arrow-key behaviour). **Status:** **verified** (the arrow keys do nothing; Tab walks the items and the menu stays open). **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:**
  - `src/ui.ts:150` (`rowMenu`) and `src/ui.ts:56` (`#kbMenu`).
  - `#kbMenu` holds a `<p>`, a `<select>` when several inputs exist, and a note, none of which a menu may contain.
  - The dock pop-ups contain switches, which is also not menu content.
- **Evidence:** `kbd-dark.log`: `ArrowDown -> … "Perform"` (no move); `Tab -> "Listen" … menu still open: true`.
- **Fix:** the fewest lines is to drop `role=menu` and `role=menuitem` and keep the disclosure: a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and a plain group of buttons. VoiceOver users then hear a disclosure button and a group of buttons. Otherwise implement Up, Down, Home and End, and close on Tab.

### A11Y-11 · The read-through look-through is a 30-second limit with no pause or extension
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable (A), unless the limit counts as essential. **Status:** verified by code. **Call:** judgement call (the limit is the exercise).
- **Where:** `src/app/readthrough-ui.ts:54-62` and `src/readthrough.ts:9` (`LOOK = 30`). The count-in starts by itself at 0. The countdown text `#rtLeft` is not in a live region, so a screen-reader user is not warned.
- **Fix:**
  - Add "More time" (+30 s) or a "No time limit" switch on the look-through card.
  - Announce "10 seconds left" once with `say()`.
  - Sight Run's Steady beat is opt-in ("Waits for me" is the default). The pulse and rhythm trainers are real-time and essential, so they are fine.

### A11Y-12 · At 200% zoom, or on a short stage, Notes shown (Simplify) disappears
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 1.4.4 and 1.4.10 (loss of functionality). **Status:** **verified.** **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:** `src/style.css:969-1015`. Container queries `stage (max-height: 420px / 260px)` set `div.shows { display:none }`, even though `#ready` is already `overflow:auto`.
- **Evidence:** `probe3`: at 590×410 and 320×600, `level: 'hidden'`; at 1180×820, it is visible. See the screenshots `zoom-590x410-startcard.png` and `zoom-320x256-startcard.png`; at 400% the start card is crushed behind the keys.
- **Fix:** let `#ready` scroll and keep the row, or move Notes shown into the dock's Practice menu, which is always reachable. The staff and highway themselves fall under 1.4.10's exception for content that needs a two-dimensional layout.

### A11Y-13 · Keyboard connection changes and banner flashes are announced unreliably
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 4.1.3. **Status:** node replacement **verified**; speech **inferred.** **Call:** clear.
- **Where:**
  - `src/app/banner.ts:38` rebuilds `<div class="banner" role="status">` on every update.
  - `flash()` (`banner.ts:16-25`) clears its message after 5 s.
  - `updateChip()` (`banner.ts:41-48`) changes the chip's name silently.
  - So "Keyboard disconnected. Plug it back in and press Space to resume." may never be spoken.
- **Fix:** in `connectMidi`'s callback, `say(\`${name} connected\`)` or `say('Keyboard disconnected…')`, and keep `#banner` itself as the one persistent `role=status` rather than its child.

### A11Y-14 · While the bar is hushed, its controls stay in the accessibility tree but cannot be activated
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 2.1.1 / 4.1.2 for VoiceOver. **Status:** **inferred** (needs a device). **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:** `src/style.css:2240-2244`. `body.hush .bar > *, #dock > *` is set to `opacity:0; pointer-events:none`.
- **Problem:** VoiceOver focuses these invisible elements, and a double-tap then dispatches a tap at their centre. With `pointer-events:none`, that tap lands on the canvas underneath, so Pause and the other controls do nothing until a real pointer move wakes the bar.
- **Fix:** wake on `focusin` as well as on pointer and key events, and drop `pointer-events:none` from the dock (its opacity alone is enough to hush it). Test with VoiceOver on the iPad.

### A11Y-15 · Empty Progress mastery cells are 1.1-1.2:1 against the page
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 1.4.11. **Status:** **verified.** **Call:** judgement call (the group caption gives "n of m solid" in text).
- **Where:** `button.cell` (`src/ui-views.ts:233, 240`), styled in `src/style.css` near `.crow` (2388). It has a Panel Raised fill and no border.
- **Evidence:** `mastery cell bg: 1.17:1` (dark) and `1.1:1` (light). These are tappable buttons, and at 0% there is nothing else to see. See `dark-1180x820-progress.png`.
- **Fix:** add a 1px `--edge` inset ring on cells: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--specular), inset 0 0 0 1px var(--edge)`.

### A11Y-16 · The drawer is modal for the keyboard but not for VoiceOver
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 1.3.1 and 2.4.3. **Status:** **verified** (`inert behind? { main: false, bar: false }`, since only `#main > section` is inert). **Call:** clear.
- **Where:**
  - `src/ui.ts:86` declares `<aside id="drawer" aria-label="Settings">`.
  - `src/app/drawer.ts:24` makes only the sections inert. The top bar stays live, so a VoiceOver swipe walks out of the drawer into the navigation.
  - The drawer heading is `<h2 role="heading" aria-level="1">` (`src/ui-drawer.ts:16`), which is also the axe `heading-order` violation.
- **Fix:**
  - Give it `role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby="drawerTitle"`, and make `.bar` inert too, except `#settingsBtn`. Alternatively, move the close action inside the drawer.
  - Drop `role="heading" aria-level="1"` from the h2.

### A11Y-17 · The notation is one static label: no piece, key, metre or bar
- **Severity:** P2. **WCAG:** 1.1.1. Sight-reading drills qualify for the test exception, but pieces do not. **Status:** **verified** (`aria-label: "Grand staff showing the notes about to be played"` even on the treble-only drill). **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:** `src/staff.ts:77`.
- **Fix:** build the label from what is known. For example: "Greensleeves, A minor, 3/4, bars 1 to 2 of 16, grand staff". Refresh it on window change, without making it live. The start card's cover is `aria-hidden` and its facts are in `#ready`, which is good.

## P3: enhancements

### A11Y-18 · axe `scrollable-region-focusable` on the drill's ready card
- **Status:** **verified**, at 1180×820 and 820×1180 in both modes. **Call:** clear.
- **Where:** `#ready` (`overflow:auto`) overflows by a few px and holds nothing focusable.
- **Fix:** add `tabindex="0"` when it scrolls, or trim its padding so it does not overflow.

### A11Y-19 · The Read stages table's band rows trigger axe `th-has-data-cells` (incomplete)
- **Status:** **verified.** **Call:** clear.
- **Where:** `src/ui-views.ts:55` puts a `<th colspan scope="colgroup">` in its own row.
- **Fix:** use `scope="rowgroup"` inside a `<tbody>` per band.

### A11Y-20 · The section switcher is a radiogroup, and there is no `<nav>` landmark or skip link
- **Status:** **verified** (the landmark list has no `nav`). **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:** `src/ui.ts:34`.
- **What is already fine:** arrow keys switch sections (verified). Only about four tab stops precede the content, so a skip link has low value.
- **Fix:** wrap it in `<nav aria-label="Sections">`. Also update `document.title` per section and per piece (it is always "Piano Forte").

### A11Y-21 · The keyboard chip's accessible name lacks its purpose
- **Status:** **verified.** **Call:** clear.
- **Where:** `src/app/banner.ts:47` sets the name to "No keyboard" or the device name. VoiceOver reads "Roland FP-10, button, menu".
- **Fix:** use `aria-label="Keyboard: ${name}"`. `#tempoBtn`'s name runs together ("Tempo100%"): add a space or an `aria-label`.

### A11Y-22 · Accompaniment noteheads are 2.5:1 on paper
- **Status:** **verified** by calculation. **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:** DESIGN.md sets accompaniment noteheads at 40% Paper Ink. That gives 2.51:1 on the Nocturne paper and 2.55:1 on Matinée, under 3:1. The dimming is the point (not your part), but the notes are still information.
- **Fix:** 50%, which gives about 3.4:1.

### A11Y-23 · Results cards are `role="dialog"` but not modal
- **Status:** **verified.** **Call:** judgement call.
- **Problem:** focus moves in correctly (to the primary, `#next`), but Tab continues into the dock and the bar behind.
- **Fix:** use `role="region"` with the same `aria-label`, or make the stage behind inert while it shows.

### A11Y-24 · The Theory on-screen keys are unusable with VoiceOver, and the alternative is only described
- **Status:** **inferred.** **Call:** judgement call.
- **Where:** `src/app/osk-ui.ts:25`. The keys are `aria-hidden`, and an sr-only note points to a MIDI keyboard or the computer keys.
- **Problem:** that is the right call for a piano grid, but an iPad VoiceOver user with neither keyboard cannot answer cards.
- **Optional fix:** in Theory, offer note-name answer buttons (C to B), which would make the ear trainers fully accessible.

### A11Y-25 · The start card title clamps under 1.4.12 text spacing
- **Status:** **verified.** **Call:** clear.
- **Where:** `b.ttl` (the start card title) is clipped by line-clamp under the text-spacing override. Nothing else on any page clipped.
- **Fix:** let it grow by a line.

## Checked and passing (keep)
- **Touch.** Every interactive element is at least 44 px under `pointer: coarse`, on all pages, sheets, menus, the dock, the start card, the Theory card with on-screen keys, and the Sight Run builder, at 1180×820 and 820×1180 (`touch.mjs`).
  - The one exception is `#split`, at 18 px tall and full width. It passes 2.5.8 through the spacing exception, and its drag has a tap alternative (2.5.7). A 44 px hit strip, with the visual staying 18 px, would satisfy the HIG.
  - Hover styles are gated by `@media (hover: hover)`.
- **Focus ring.** The 2 px Accent ring shows on every control, including segment cells (via `input:focus-visible + span`), switches and the slider thumb (`focus-slider-sClick.png`). Its contrast is 6.4-8.2:1 in Nocturne and 5.0-6.4:1 in Matinée on every surface.
- **Focus return and traps.**
  - Help, Credits and Set up are native `showModal`; focus starts on Close and returns to the opener.
  - The drawer wraps Tab, and Escape returns focus to the Settings button.
  - The ⋯ row menu and the chip panel return focus to their button on Escape.
  - Opening a piece focuses Play, and results focus the primary action.
- **Keyboard.** The segmented radios use native arrow keys. `#split` is a focusable separator with arrow keys and `aria-valuenow`. A Skip link in the Songs list jumps past 50 rows. No keyboard trap was found.
- **Colour.** Text contrast passes everywhere axe could compute it, and `palette.test.ts` covers the token pairs.
  - Judgements carry a glyph and a word (● ▲ ▸ ×, plus the word).
  - The hands differ by fill style as well as hue (left outlined and tinted, right solid).
  - Staff marks have shapes. Hold outlines are now solid versus dashed, so the "hue only" item in DESIGN.md's debt list is stale.
- **Motion.** The canvas honours Reduce Motion (no glow swell, a steady wait ring), as do the staff roll (`staff.ts:116`) and the judge popup (`stage.ts:317`).
  - Flash risk is low: glows are one lane wide and far under 25% of the field, and the wait-ring pulse is about 1.1 Hz.
- **Live regions.** Apart from A11Y-8, play is quiet: no per-note judgements are spoken, and results, pause and tempo changes are each said once.
- **iPad.**
  - The menu bar (`AppDelegate.swift:18-44`) exposes ⌘ shortcuts in the hold-⌘ list.
  - The `?` Help sheet lists every shortcut.
  - `preferredScreenEdgesDeferringSystemGestures` is only active while playing.
  - Safe areas are respected (`style.css:175`). Hiding the status bar is a design choice, not an accessibility issue.
- **Reflow.** At 200% and 400%, no page scrolls horizontally, and pages reflow with no clipped text. The nav strip scrolls on its own.

## Recommended order
1. A11Y-1, A11Y-2, A11Y-3 and A11Y-8 are small, verified and in the core loop. Each is a few lines.
2. A11Y-4 and A11Y-13 route status text through `say()` and wrap re-renders in `keepFocus()`; both helpers already exist.
3. A11Y-6 is a one-token change, plus the matching test pairs.
4. A11Y-5 is one setting that gates the shortcuts.
5. A11Y-7 (Dynamic Type) is the largest change. Plan it as its own pass, starting with `-apple-system-body` and a root scale.
6. Then the P2s, then `/impeccable polish`.
