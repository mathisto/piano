Scope: every user-facing string in `src/*.ts` and `src/app/*.ts` (labels, buttons, headings, hints, empty states, errors, confirmations, results and coaching lines, aria-labels, tooltips, Help, Credits, Set up, first-run, banners, toasts). Read against PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md (Copy rules, Two Voices, Do/Don't) and docs/research/urtext.md. A sample of claims was checked against the code; where a finding says "the code does X", the file is cited. Screenshots of the rendered app (1180x820, coarse pointer) are in `scratchpad/audit/copy/`.

Nothing in the repo was edited.

Severity: **P1** wrong or confusing (the copy misleads, is broken, or says something the code does not do); **P2** inconsistent (two words for one thing, one word for two things, units, old names); **P3** polish.
Each finding is marked **clear** (apply as written) or **judgement call** (needs Matt's decision; usually a product or naming choice).

Counts: **P1 17 · P2 34 · P3 30 · total 81** (clear 52, judgement 29).

## P1: wrong or confusing

### CP-1 · P1 · clear
`src/ui-drawer.ts:25`
Current: "Your keyboard's own speakers. The app stays quiet except for the count-in and metronome."
Proposed: "Your keyboard's speakers play what you press. The app still sounds what it plays itself: Listen, the parts it plays for you, ear questions, the count-in and the metronome."
Why: not true. Listen, accompaniment and lead-sheet chords force sound (`game.ts:355`, `game.ts:362`), so do the ear trainers (`ear-play.ts:61`) and the on-screen and computer keys (`input.ts:24`). A player on Keyboard who hears the app play is told it should not.

### CP-2 · P1 · clear
`src/ui-drawer.ts:19`
Current: "How close to the beat a note must land to count. Easy allows about a third more time; Strict a quarter less, and also checks how long you hold each note."
Proposed: "How close to the beat a note must land to count. Easy allows about a third more time; Strict a quarter less. In Perform, Standard and Strict also check how long you hold each note; Easy does not."
Why: `game.ts:59` sets holds to off for Easy, lenient for Standard and strict for Strict, and `game.ts:180` judges holds only in Perform. The current line implies only Strict checks holds.
