**Specificity verdict:** the look is grounded in the product, not generic. The paper staff, engraved covers, the Pause mark and the single lamp could not belong to another app. Most of what is wrong is continuity and flow, not identity.

**Can a new user tell what to do first?** Not reliably. First run gives three different "first things" (UX-6). Day 1's step then opens a drill whose instruction is invisible on an iPad without a keyboard (UX-4). If the user picks the lamp instead, they get an exercise card labelled "LOCAL IMPORT · MIDI" and "Scored" (UX-5, UX-22). And no screen shows a way back except tapping the logo (UX-3).

## Findings, most severe first

Each finding gives its surface, its location, the issue, the evidence, a proposed fix, and whether the fix is **clear** (a reasonable designer would just do it) or a **judgement call** for the owner.

### UX-1 · P0 · The start card breaks in Staff view
- **Surface:** the play screen in Staff view, at every size.
- **Where:** `src/style.css:959-961` (`#play.v-sheet` gives the stage `clamp(96px, 15vh, 150px)`); the start card rules under the "short stage" comment at `src/style.css:968` onward.
- **Issue:** the start card is squeezed into the 96 to 150 px strip of keys.
  - The cover is cut off (its title and "Beginner" are clipped at the top).
  - Perform | Learn, Notes shown, the hint and the legend fall outside the card. Measured: `#ready` is 87 px tall against 155 px of content, and Perform | Learn sits at y=590, above the card's top at y=636.
  - The card prints over the keys.
  - The top bar's Perform | Learn is hidden while the start card shows, so in Staff view no visible control chooses the mode.
- **Evidence:** `ux/p02-start-perform-staff-L-dark.png`, `ux/t-start-staff-P-dark.png`, `ux/t-start-staff-S-dark.png`.
- **Fix:** while the run is in the ready state and the strip is shorter than the card needs, lay the start card over the paper (top-centred on the staff, which is static before Play), not inside the strip. As a minimum, show the top bar's Perform | Learn whenever the card cannot hold it.
- **Call:** clear.

### UX-2 · P1 · Escape in a dock menu leaves the piece
- **Surface:** the dock's Tempo menu, very likely also the results ⋯ menu and the keyboard chip panel on the play screen (inferred, same pattern).
- **Where:** `src/dock.ts:146-151` (the Escape listener is on `.popmenu`, so it only fires when focus is inside the menu); `src/app/input.ts:56-59` (the page's Escape calls `leave()`).
- **Issue:** a tap or click on Tempo leaves focus on the button. Pressing Escape then skips the menu's handler and the page's handler leaves the piece. Verified: after Escape, `#play.on` is false. The e2e test only covers Escape after moving focus into the Aids menu, so it passes.
- **Evidence:** probe log in `ux-scripts/probe.mjs` ("after Escape in Tempo menu, still on play: false"). The menu itself: `ux/p11-tempo-menu-L-dark.png`.
- **Fix:** move the listener to the `.pop` wrapper (button and menu), or have `input.ts` close any open `.popmenu:not([hidden])` or `#kbMenu` and return before `leave()`. Add the button-focused case to e2e feature 23.
- **Call:** clear.

### UX-3 · P1 · No visible way back from a piece on touch
- **Surface:** the play screen for songs, Technique and Scales runs, and calibration.
- **Where:** `src/main.ts:85` (the mark is the only route); `src/app/play.ts:116` (the mark is renamed "leave this piece" for screen readers only).
- **Issue:** the top bar holds the mark, the title, the HUD, the chip and the settings button. Nothing says Back. Reading drills get a labelled "All stages" or "Finish" button, so the same screen type behaves two ways. A first-time adult will not guess that the logo leaves, and Help documents only "Esc: Back".
- **Evidence:** `ux/p01-start-perform-both-L-dark.png` and `ux/p34-technique-start-L-dark.png`; compare `ux/p21-drill-start-L-dark.png` (with "All stages").
- **Fix:** put a labelled back button between the mark and the title, the drills' "All stages" button reused, named after where it returns ("Songs", "Technique", "Scales"). Keep the mark as a second route.
- **Call:** clear.

### UX-5 · P1 · Exercise and scale covers read "LOCAL IMPORT · MIDI"
- **Surface:** the start card for Technique exercises and Scales stages, and for the plan rows that open them.
- **Where:** `src/app/play.ts:244`. For a song with no catalogue or starter entry it falls back to `covers.fileMeta('midi', title)`.
- **Issue:** the cover header says "LOCAL IMPORT · MIDI" and "KEY — · METRE —" for an exercise the app generated itself, whose key and metre it knows. This is false metadata on the most visible surface of a run, and it is the first thing the Today lamp opens on day 1.
