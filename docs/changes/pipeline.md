# Library batch pipeline (scripts/library/)

`npm run library:build -- --set mutopia|current [--grades 1-4] [--limit 500] [--out DIR]` bundles `pipeline.ts` with the app's importers (vite ssr, no new dependencies), then runs:

1. **fetch** (`build.mjs`, `lib.mjs`, `mutopia-index.mjs`): Mutopia's search table for Piano, Harpsichord and Clavichord (113 pages), then each piece page, `.mid` and `.ly`. One request a second, the existing User-Agent, every response cached under `.cache/library/http/<sha256(url)>`, so a rerun is offline (27 s) and resumes where it stopped.
2. **licence**: read from the piece's own page (Mutopia) or the file's `<rights>` (OpenScore). Allowed: `scripts/library-sources.json` `allow` plus MutopiaBSD. Composer must have died in or before year minus 71; the latest year named by the edition or composition date must be year minus 96 or earlier. The `current` set also checks the sha256 pins and the licence and dates in the sources.
3. **normalise**: Mutopia solo keyboard only (every listed instrument is piano, harpsichord, clavichord...); MusicXML keeps the keyboard parts (or the `dropParts`/`foldParts` in the sources).
   A work offered only as a `-mids.zip` is split into one piece a movement: `mutopia-N-k`, in LilyPond's file order (`name.mid`, `name-1.mid`, ...), titled `Title: Allemande` when the file name carries a dance name, else `Title: Movement k`; four-hand files are left out; each movement reads its own `.ly` from the `-lys.zip` when one has the same name, else the work's `.ly`.
4. **analyse** (`pipeline.ts`): `fromMidi`/`fromMusicXml`, gates, flags, tags, grade (`GATES`, `judge`). MIDI with 3+ tracks gets `pick` (hands by track name, else middle pitch). Repeats, D.C. and tempo marks come from the `.ly` for Mutopia MIDI; `repeats` is not flagged when the `.ly` has `\unfoldRepeats` (its MIDI already plays them) or for MusicXML unless the importer says `repeats-unexpanded`.
5. **dedupe**: same composer (surname and dates), plus the same catalogue number, title words Jaccard >= 0.6 or the same Mutopia page, confirmed by top-line interval 4-grams Jaccard >= 0.6. A **pinned** id (the 38 in `scripts/library-sources.json`, i.e. `public/library`) always wins; in the mutopia set those pieces also keep their curated title and sha256 pin, and ship at any grade.
6. **select**: quality >= 70, grades in `--grades`, then at most `--limit`: pinned first, then by grade and D, so grade 4 only fills what grades 1 to 3 leave.
7. **emit**: `.cache/library/out/{catalog.json,LICENSES.md,p/<sha16>.mid|.mxl}`, sorted, no timestamps; `aliases` maps a dropped duplicate's id to the shipped winner (never a pinned id: that one is the bundle's). The outcome for every candidate is in `.cache/library/report-<set>.json`.

**Grade**: seven capped terms (onset rate, busy-bar notes, chord size, hand span, range, accidentals, tuplets and sixteenths) summed to D, unchanged; `CUTS` in `pipeline.ts` turn D into grades 1 to 5 and are the 8th/30th/60th/85th percentiles of D over the analysed Mutopia set. `node scripts/library/fit.mjs` (after both builds) prints fresh cut points, the Spearman with the hand labels in `scripts/library/labels.json` (159 pieces, one-line reason each: the 38 bundled pieces, Anna Magdalena book, Burgmueller Op. 100, Schumann Op. 68 and Op. 15, Tchaikovsky Op. 39, Kuhlau, Czerny, Bach inventions and WTC, sonata movements, Chopin, Joplin, Satie...), and how each label landed.

Types: `src/catalog-types.ts`. Grade overrides: `scripts/library/overrides.json` (`{"mutopia-75": 1}`). `fetch-library.mjs` now imports its helpers from `lib.mjs` (same behaviour). Tests: `src/library-pipeline.test.ts`.

## Runs (2026-10-27)

- **current** (38): all 38 ship, ids and sha256 identical to `public/library/manifest.json`. Grades 1:7, 2:11, 3:6, 4:13, 5:1. Beginner pieces grade 1 to 2, Elementary 1 to 4, Intermediate 2 to 5.
- **mutopia**: 827 keyboard-tagged pieces, **638 solo keyboard**. Refused: 75 composer death unknown (57 Traditional, 13 living composers who licensed their own work, 5 without dates), 3 more on dates, 2 zip works with no MIDI to read (665: the zip is a 404; 2207 Debussy Preludes: no MIDI at all). 27 zip works split into 254 movements. **785 analysed**, 2 fail (`bars`: two Clementi lessons under 4 bars), 28 duplicates (Pictures and French Suite 3 carry each movement twice in their zips; BWV 928 twice; `mutopia-921` now loses to the pinned `mutopia-468`), 1 under quality 70. Grades over the analysed set **1:61 (7.8%), 2:174 (22%), 3:236 (30%), 4:196 (25%), 5:118 (15%)**. **500 shipped**: grade 1:61, 2:168, 3:225, 4:45 (the easiest by D), 5:1 (a pinned piece); all 33 pinned Mutopia pieces ship with their bundled title and sha256; 17 aliases. 132 shipped pieces keep the `repeats` flag. Warm run 32 s, byte-identical rerun. catalog.json 363 KB, 45 KB brotli (89 B a row).
- **Grade fit**: Spearman with the 159 labels is 0.848 on D and **0.822 on the grade** (0.821 with the old cut points: the terms already ranked well, the cut points were too low; a coordinate search over term weights, scaled by 5th to 95th percentiles, gained under 0.01 and held out at 0.83 to 0.85, so it was dropped). Against the labels the new grades read about half a step easy (label 3 lands on 2 or 3, label 4 on 3 or 4), because the target distribution asks Mutopia's advanced-heavy set for 60% at grades 1 to 3; cut points chosen to match the labels instead were about [2.6, 4.75, 6.9, 7.7], which put 3/14/35/18/30% in grades 1 to 5.

## Open

- **Repeats**: unfolded at ingestion since 2026-10-27 (`repeats.ts`, `docs/changes/repeats.md`); 38 shipped pieces keep the flag (13 of them bundled).
- Grade calibration: the cut points follow the asked distribution, not the labels (see above). Decide which the app wants before the bundle is cut; a label-matched set is one `fit.mjs` change.
- Movement titles: `Movement k` follows the zip's file order, which is the score order for LilyPond's numbered files; the Partitas' `51title-hub.mid` style and Clementi's lesson files read as numbers too. Dedupe can merge two different movements of one work whose top lines share most 4-grams (Pictures' Promenades; the last two of BWV 827 have the same D).
- `catKey` reads Mutopia's opus field before the title, so `Op. 28` matches every Prelude of Op. 28; the fingerprint keeps them apart, but the key is coarser than it looks.
- Policy calls for the owner: Traditional tunes (57) and living composers' own CC/PD releases (13) are refused by the death-year rule; CC 2.5 licences are allowed because the project allowlist has them.
