# Humdrum **kern: the Chopin Institute's first editions (src/kern.ts, build.mjs --set chopin)

**Converter.** `kernToMusicXml(src, rights?)` turns a **kern score into a partwise MusicXML with one piano part on two staves (the last `**kern` spine, or `*staff1`, is the upper staff and the right hand); `fromKern` is `fromMusicXml` of that, so the .mxl the library ships is exactly what was analysed and the round trip is the identity by construction. Timing is line-based as in humlib: a line lasts until the first note on it or still sounding ends, and a spine whose note just ended holds the clock only on a line where nothing starts (a null or **dynam line lasts 0). It reads pitch by case and repetition (c is C4, cc C5, C C3, CC C2), `#`/`-`/`n`, durations with dots, reciprocal tuplets (12, 20, 68...) and `N%M` (written type and `<time-modification>` are worked out from the value), rests (a rest with a pitch is still a rest; `yy` and a bare duration are invisible spacers), chords, ties `[ _ ]`, grace notes `q`/`qq`, slurs, articulations, `*k[]`, `*M`, `*MM` (or the `!!!OMD` number, reading [quarter], [half], [half-dot], [quarter-dot], [eighth]; OMD words go in as tempo words), `*clef`, **dynam letters and hairpins (the importer turns them into velocity), `*^`/`*v` (each subspine a voice of its staff), pickups and short bars (`implicit`). A `*>[A,A,B]` expansion list is played out (a file with no labels keeps its barline repeats, which go into the score for the importer); the short bar before a repeat and the pickup after it are joined into one bar. A note that overruns its barline is clipped there. Ignored: beams, stems, layout, ornaments, **fing, **text, `*rscale`, ottava marks (kern writes sounding pitch) and `*cue` (Chopin's small-note cadenzas are played).
**Strict.** An error, not wrong notes: more than two `**kern` spines (staves), `*+`, `*x`, a line whose tokens do not match its spines, an expansion label with no bars, a section label inside a bar. A token it cannot read (`16edeeLL`) keeps its time as a hidden rest and is counted in `skipped`; build.mjs refuses a file that loses over 1% of its notes that way. A chord note with no duration takes the one before it (counted).

**Set.** `node scripts/library/build.mjs --set chopin [--merge-with <published>]`: the git tree of `pl-wnifc/humdrum-chopin-first-editions`, then `raw.githubusercontent.com`, one request a second, cached in `.cache/library/http`. `LICENSE.txt` must name CC BY 4.0 (else the set is refused); its first five lines, the credit it asks for, go into each file's `<rights>`. Part files of chamber and four-hand works (`-piano`, `-violino`, `-violoncello`, `-piano-primo`...) are refused; the composer must be Chopin (d. 1849, so every first edition passes the 1930 rule in `refusals()`). Title from `!!!rism-title` ("Nocturne in E-flat major, Op. 9 No. 2"), with ": Movement N" when one edition has several files of one work, else OTL plus OPS; opus from `rism-opus`. Catalogue: `source: 'chopin'` (credit "Fryderyk Chopin Institute (chopinscores.org)"), `edition` "First edition: Kistner, Leipzig (title page); digital edition © The Fryderyk Chopin Institute (nifc.pl), chopinscores.org", `typesetter` the encoder(s) from `!!!ENC`, licence CC-BY-4.0, evidence the repo's LICENSE.txt.
**One edition a work.** dedupe as for every set (catalogue number or title, then the top-line fingerprint); between equally good versions the new `Input.rank` decides: the first impression before a later issue (Rink-Grabowski `1` before `1a`, `1b`), then Paris (Chopin read the proofs of most French editions), Leipzig or Berlin, London (Wessel mostly reprinted Paris), the rest. With `--merge-with`, the published Chopin pieces join dedupe pinned, so a work the library already has is not shipped twice.

**src beyond the importer**: `catalog-types.ts` gains `'chopin'` in `Piece['source']` and `credits.ts` exports `SOURCE` with its label (pipeline.ts's LICENSES.md uses the same map), else the Credits sheet would print "undefined". `pipeline.ts` re-exports `kernToMusicXml` (the bundle build.mjs loads).

## Run (2026-10-27, cold fetch 515 requests, warm run 18 s, byte-identical rerun)

512 files: **124 normalise** (62 three-staff and 8 four-staff scores: the 48 Op. 74 songs, Op. 8 Trio, Op. 65, Grand Duo, Opp. 2 and 14 with orchestra; 51 chamber or four-hand parts; 3 structure errors), **388 analysed**, 0 fail kept (the two E-flat0 `pitch` fails lost dedupe to a sibling edition), **208 duplicates** (142 of another edition, 66 of a published Mutopia piece), 2 under quality 70: **178 ship**, grades 2: 3, 3: 66, 4: 36, 5: 73. Flags among them: no-tempo 83, tuplets 60, bar-length 26 (cadenza bars), key 21 (modulating pieces; every signature matched `rism-key`), span 12, length 4, chord 3.
**Union** `.cache/library/out-kern` (`--merge-with` the published out-final): **1,988 pieces** (1,810 + 178), grades 1: 71, 2: 282, 3: 618, 4: 547, 5: 470. The 1,810 entries, composer and licence tables, 29 aliases and all base files are identical; 205 new aliases (dropped editions to the shipped one), none dangling; every file's sha256 and size checked; catalog.json 1.61 MB.
**Accuracy against Mutopia** (52 first-edition and Mutopia pairs of the same work, notes as quarter onset and pitch): median F1 0.985, 36 at 0.95 or more, pitch alone 47 at 0.95 or more. The rest, read bar by bar: Mutopia's MIDI quantiser straightened the left-hand triplets in Op. 28 No. 8, edition differences and cadenzas (Op. 9, Op. 28 Nos. 18 and 24), and Mazurka Op. 6 No. 1, whose file's own expansion list plays the repeats Mutopia leaves out (113 bars to 74).

- Op. 9 No. 2: `!!!SIC` says the printed M.M. should be an eighth, not a quarter; SIC records are not read (the published Mutopia version ships instead).
- A key or clef change written inside a bar takes effect from the next bar (the app draws its own clefs; keys only affect spelling).
- 83 shipped pieces have only a tempo word (the importer's table picks the bpm) and keep the `no-tempo` flag.
