# OpenScore Lieder in the batch pipeline (scripts/library/)

`npm run library:build -- --set lieder --out .cache/library/out-lieder --merge-with <published out>` (defaults: every grade, no limit, since `docs/changes/libgrow.md`).

- **Fetch**: the repo tree from the GitHub API (one request), `data/composers.yaml` (names, birth and death years by folder), then every `scores/**/lc*.mxl` (1,462). Same polite `get()`: one request a second, the project User-Agent, cached in `.cache/library/http`; a warm rerun takes 41 s and its output is byte-identical.
- **Licence**: the file's `<rights>` must say CC0 (`openscoreMeta`), plus the usual death and edition-year rule (`refusals`).
- **Normalise** (`build.mjs liederSet`): parts are read by `scoreParts` (id, name, staves, lyrics). Exactly one keyboard part on two staves is needed. A part that is not a keyboard and has no lyrics, or that has an instrument name (flute, viola, clarinet...), is refused as an obbligato. With exactly one vocal part on one staff, the song is tried **folded** (`<staves>2</staves>` on the voice, as for the bundled songs) with its piano-only version as `alt`. Duets, choirs and a voice on several staves go **piano-only**. Ids are `lieder-<file stem>`, as in the bundle. The five bundled songs are pinned and keep their curated titles and fold choice.
- **Fold gate** (`pipeline.ts reach`, `voiced`): over the right-hand onsets of the folded song (doubled notes are already merged by the importer), the 95th-percentile reach must be 14 semitones or less and no onset may hold more than 5 notes. The folded version must also pass the normal gates (no fail, quality >= 70). If it does not, the piano-only version is analysed and ships only if it passes the normal gates itself. The report keeps the reason, e.g. `voice dropped: folded right-hand reach 17, 5 at once`.
- **Flags and titles**: `voice-folded` / `voice-dropped` (added after `judge`, so they cost no quality); `<Title>, Op. N No. k: melody and piano` or `: piano part`, the opus taken from the set folder. Edition text no longer carries "; the vocal line is folded..." (the flag says it, as `builtin()` does). LICENSES.md's credit column names the change.
- **Merge** (`pipeline.ts merge`): every base piece, `p/` file, composer and licence row, and alias stays byte-for-byte as it was. New pieces go after them, and their composer and licence are re-indexed into the base tables (a composer is matched by surname and death year, so `J. Brahms` is reused). A new id the base already has is skipped. `built` is sha256(base built + run built). Publish with `LIBRARY_OUT=.cache/library/out-lieder scripts/deploy-library.sh`, which only adds files.

## Run (2026-10-27)

1,462 files. Refused: 75 at normalise (43 instrument parts, 25 with no piano (choirs, voice and guitar), 5 with two piano parts, 2 with a piano part not on two staves), 40 at licence (34 composer death unknown, 6 without CC0 in `<rights>`). That leaves **1,347 analysed**: 1,251 tried folded, 88 piano-only from the start, 3 with no voice, 5 pinned. 871 folds passed the gate and 380 failed: 244 on reach alone, 51 on more than 5 notes alone, 77 on both, 8 on the normal gates. The 88 piano-only songs have 2 to 7 vocal parts or a voice on several staves. Then 4 failed `pitch`, 1 duplicate, 4 under quality 70, 370 at grade 5 and 668 over the limit.

**300 shipped**: 188 folded, 112 piano-only. Grades 1:12, 2:124, 3:162, 4:2 (both pinned). Grade 3 is cut by the limit (406 grade 3 analysed). Analysed overall 1:12, 2:124, 3:406, 4:430, 5:375: Lieder accompaniments run harder than Mutopia. Merged: 800 pieces, 66 new composers (132 in all), CC0 added as the 7th licence, 21 aliases (the base's 20 and one new). The 500 base pieces, the tables' prefixes, the aliases and all 500 base files are identical.

Checked through the real importer (`src/library-pipeline.test.ts`): the bundled folded songs and the first three new ones pass the gate and hold more right-hand notes than piano-only (Du liebes Auge 144 vs 125, Liebesklage des Mädchens 352 vs 287, Die Liebe des Nächsten 158 vs 157: there the piano already doubles the tune). The fallback and the merge are tested on a fixture.

## Open

- **Publication year is not checked**: 36 shipped songs are by composers who died between 1931 and 1955 (Strauss, Schoenberg, Webern, Zemlinsky, Bantock, Beach, Bridge, Chaminade...). The Lieder metadata has no publication year, and the editions rarely name one, so the "published 1930 or earlier" half of the world-safe test rests on the IMSLP source of each song. Read the IMSLP pages, or refuse composers who died after 1930 unless a year is found, before publishing.
- The fold gate fails mostly on reach (a voice an octave or more under the right hand: tenor and baritone songs). Transposing the voice up an octave when it sits below the right hand would save many of these; not done.
- The `reasons` console summary lists each reach value separately; the report JSON is the place to read them.
