# Chopin's Op. 74 (the Polish songs) from the Chopin Institute's first editions

Branch `feature/op74`. Voice and piano, the way OpenScore Lieder is done (`docs/changes/lieder.md`, `voice.md`).

## What changed
- **`src/kern.ts`**: three `**kern` spines plus a `**text` spine is a song: the top staff (`*staff1`, the lowest number) is the voice, the other two the piano. `kernToMusicXml` still returns `xml` (the piano part alone) and now `sung`: the same score with a second part `Voice` declared on `<staves>2</staves>`, which the importer plays with the right hand (the fold of `openscoreMeta`, so `voiceOf` puts it on the strings). A `**dynam` belongs to the staff of the `**kern` on its left, so the piano's and the voice's hairpins and letters each go to their own part (`Bar.dirs[].v`); tempo and words stay with the piano part, the importer's first. Three staves with no `**text`, and four, are still refused (14 and 8 files: the Trio, the cello sonata, the Grand Duo and the orchestral Opp. 2 and 14). **Lyrics are not read**: the importer has no use for `<lyric>`, so emitting them would be dead data.
- **`scripts/library/build.mjs`** (chopin set): a file with `sung` becomes two inputs as in `liederSet`, the folded one (`: melody and piano`, `voice: 'folded'`) with the piano-only one (`: piano part`, `voice: 'dropped'`) as `alt`; `pipeline.ts voiced()` takes the fold gate (`reach`) from there. Titles come from the file number (the three editions number alike, and no file names its song number reliably): `Wiosna (Spring), Op. 74 No. 2: melody and piano`, Polish title and the English one of en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_songs_(Chopin). The title uses the Lieder colon, not a dash. A song outside `074-` is refused rather than given no title. Tie-break rank: Gebethner (Warsaw, the Polish text) before the Berlin and London translations. The composer check is `/chop/i` because two files spell it `Chopipn`.
- **`scripts/library/pipeline.ts dedupe`**: two voiced inputs with one catalogue number (`Op. 74 No. 4`) are one song whatever their fingerprints, because one edition can fold and another fall back to piano-only (No. 4 shipped twice before this). Plain pieces are unchanged.
- Tests: `src/kern.test.ts` (Wiosna bars 1 to 3 verbatim, a voice of its own, the refusals), `src/library-pipeline.test.ts` (dedupe).

## Run (2026-10-27)
`node scripts/library/build.mjs --set chopin --out .cache/library/out-op74 --merge-with <main>/.cache/library/out-kern` (cold fetch of the 512 files into this worktree's own cache, warm rerun 24 s).

48 Op. 74 files (16 songs x Gebethner Warsaw, Schlesinger Berlin, Stanley Lucas Weber London; the 17th song is not in the data) all convert, 0 skipped tokens but 150 chord notes with no duration in No. 1 (they take the one before, as everywhere). Dedupe keeps the best of the three by the gates, then Warsaw: **16 songs added, one per song**, 32 aliases. Warsaw for 14, Berlin for No. 15 (grade 5 and quality 100 against Warsaw 97), London for No. 6 (quality 90 against 80).
- **Folded (11)**: Nos. 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16. **Piano-only (5)**: Nos. 5, 8, 9, 12, 13, all `voice dropped: folded right-hand reach 15 to 19, 4 or 5 at once` (a voice an octave or more away from the right hand, as the Lieder tenors and baritones). Wiosna's voice doubles the right hand, so the importer merges it and the fold adds 3 notes.
- Grades 2: 4, 3: 7, 4: 4, 5: 1. Flags: `voice-folded` 11, `voice-dropped` 5; review flags bar-length (Nos. 1, 6), span (14), key (9).
- Nothing held back for licence or year: Chopin d. 1849, the editions are 1859 and after (1930 rule passes), CC BY 4.0 from the repo's `LICENSE.txt` (evidence link), `<rights>` carries its first five lines in every file, typesetter = the `!!!ENC` encoder (Antoni Szymański for Warsaw; Wojciech Jakubiak-Danek for Nos. 6 and 15).
- Union catalogue `.cache/library/out-op74`: **2,004 pieces** (1,988 + 16). The 1,988 entries (as a list prefix), composers (158), licences (8), the base aliases and every base file (sha256 and size, byte comparison against `out-kern`) are identical. No new composer or licence.
- Converter regression: all 388 non-song files that convert give byte-identical MusicXML to HEAD; the 25 that error, error identically.
- Opened with `fromMusicXml` (throwaway test): all 16 parse, 28 to 104 bars; the 11 folded have parts `["Piano","Voice"]`, 3 to 153 voice notes beyond the doubled ones, right-hand reach 0 to 14; tempi agree with the printed M.M. (No. 2 `[quarter-dot] = 69` is 69 a beat in 6/8).

## Open
- The five piano-only songs would fold if the voice were moved an octave toward the right hand (see lieder.md); not done.
- The voice of a song that doubles the right hand adds almost nothing once merged; the `voice-folded` flag still says folded.
- Lyrics (`**text`) are ignored; Song No. 17 (Śpiew z mogiły) is not in the Institute's first editions.
- Tempo: the 16 shipped were read against their printed M.M. (all agree); the 32 dropped editions were not.
