## Evidence and attribution

The library pipeline records, for every score: title, composer, licence, licence URL, source edition, credit, source page, source hash and distributed-file hash. It rejects missing licence evidence. The generated manifest is `.cache/library/out-fermata/LICENSES.md` (2,663 lines, about 1 MB); it is the attachment to provide if App Review requests documentation.

The in-app Credits view exposes the applicable licence and source for catalogue pieces. CC BY and CC BY-SA files retain their required credit. Converted or edited CC BY-SA copies remain under the same licence.

Primary sources include:

- Mutopia Project source pages and legal terms.
- OpenScore and OpenScore Lieder score-level rights metadata.
- The Chopin Institute first-edition corpus under CC BY 4.0.
- Public-domain editions whose source page and edition are recorded per file.

## Owner-imported files

The app also lets a person import their own MIDI or MusicXML file. Those files remain local, or in Page to Hand's iCloud Drive Documents folder if the person enables sync. They are not published, uploaded to Page to Hand or added to the catalogue.
