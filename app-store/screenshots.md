3. **See what slipped—and what to do next.** A deterministic demonstration run with score marks and result card.
4. **Bring the music you already own.** An actual imported MIDI score open in the player.
5. **Build the skill behind the piece.** The Read path and its curriculum.
6. **Own the app. Keep your practice.** Settings at the local backup and iCloud Drive sync controls.

The first three carry the complete search-result story: plan, play, improve. Captions name outcomes; visible UI proves them. No screenshot claims grading, dynamic fingering, AI or any private-research feature.
