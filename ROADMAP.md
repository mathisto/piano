# Roadmap

Where the work stands. The reasoning is in `docs/STRATEGY.md`; the evidence is in `docs/research/`; each feature branch left a note in `docs/changes/` with what it built, how it was checked and what is still open. This file has five parts: Shipped, Next (with the decisions pending for the owner), Known gaps, Content and licences, Research. Counts below were read on 2026-10-27: 54 test files, 723 tests passing (1 skipped); 33 end-to-end features in the default run; 2,241 pieces in the live catalogue.

## Shipped

Grouped by area. Detail lives in the notes named at the end of each line; `docs/README.md` indexes them.

**Play**
- Perform (count-in, accuracy and notes in a row, judgement) and Learn (wait mode, tempo, A-B loop, hands separate or dimmed or heard, Hide played, Hold to advance).
- A draggable divider between the staff and the falling notes (drag to an end for one pane alone). Staff zoom by pinch, trackpad pinch, - and +, or corner buttons that can be dragged anywhere; a second line appears once two fit; Fit shows the whole piece and a double tap resets.
- Portrait and split-view windows (no minimum width), count-in click, ghost notes, held-chord readout, hold and pedal grading, strictness (Easy, Standard, Strict), calibration, `M` to hide extras.
- Error-targeted loop after every run (the two worst bars at 70%, then lose the wait, then the tempo ladder), Auto tempo in loops (+5% after three clean passes, -10% after two messy ones), an exit ramp, and a fix-it chain (three clean passes add the next bar). Clean passes are counted per loop pass.
- Read through: a 30-second look-through then one no-stop pass of a new, easy piece two levels below yours; summary with slipped bars, Fix these and Next piece (`docs/changes/readthrough.md`). The 20, 30 and 45 minute plans list it.
- Memorise (`src/memory.ts`, `docs/changes/memory.md`): Learn with the score fading by bars (stage 0 full, 1 every 4th bar hidden, 2 every 2nd, 3 barlines only, 4 no staff), a look-first facts card, Peek, weak spots; two clean passes climb a stage, more than three slips drop one; stages 3 and 4 feed spaced review. Started from a Songs row or "Try it from memory" after a clean Learn run.
- Accompaniment practice (`src/accomp.ts`, `docs/changes/accomp.md`): "You play" on the start card; the app plays the other part (a Lied's voice or piano, the other hand, a duet part) through the sampler and never waits, its notes dimmed to 40% on the staff and in the lanes; only your part is judged, and a part run records heat and history but does not set Best or count toward Ready. Notes keep their source part (`Note.part`, `Note.also`, `Song.parts`); hands are unchanged. A part named like a voice sounds on the strings sample set (`voiceOf`, `docs/changes/voice.md`).
- Recorder (`src/take.ts`, `src/app/take-ui.ts`, `docs/changes/recorder.md`): every Perform and Learn run that reaches the end is a take (the last three per piece, in IndexedDB, in the export). The results card offers Listen back (the take through the app's piano at its own timing and velocity, any key stops), Compare with last time (right keys and mean timing per bar, Hear last time), and a start-card switch for a Ghost of the last run as dim dots on the staff and traces in the lanes.
- Score marks on the staff (`src/layout.ts`, `src/staff.ts`, `docs/changes/marks.md`): staccato, tenuto and accents off the stem in a space, slurs per hand clear of the notes (open at a system edge), hairpins and the score's own dynamic letters between the staves, all at 70% ink; MusicXML only. `cresc.`, `dim.` and their long forms in `<words>` become hairpins (`docs/changes/voice.md`).
- Sustain pedal (`src/pedal.ts`, `docs/changes/pedal.md`): CC64 reaches the sampler (a released key keeps ringing while the pedal is down; the app's own notes ignore it) and, in a Perform or Learn run without Wait or a loop, the results gain one plain "Pedal:" sentence (chord changes it was changed with, bars where the old chord rang under the new one). Technique has a "Pedal changes" exercise. Built against synthetic CC64 only.
- Practice metronome (`src/metronome.ts`, `docs/changes/metro.md`): dock button and `B` cycle off, beats, beats with eighths; accent on the bar's first beat, compound meters count dotted quarters, beat dots, click timing from the audio output timestamp. The tempo ladder (Auto tempo) is a visible "Ladder on/off" dock button once a loop is set in Learn.

**Read**
- 37 sight-reading stages (`docs/changes/readcur.md`): landmarks, octave anchors, one and two ledger lines, mixed-clef cards before the grand staff, 8va, triads, sharps and flats, minor keys and keys to four flats or sharps, melody stages in several keys and the bass clef, and read-ahead tunes; weighted deck, mastery meters, typical time. A Fluent tier (1.5 s for single notes and tunes, half the goal for intervals and triads) follows Solid with a "For speed" action. The isolated-note stages stay (landmarks and the base for review).
- Sight Run (`src/run.ts`): a run you build on the Read page: clef, range, naturals, sharps, flats or a key, notes, intervals or triads, a Jumps slider, a Shuffle slider (even to weighted by your weak notes), endless or 10 to 100 cards, reading ahead (only the next 1 to 4 notes show), saved runs. Intervals and triads stack the chosen key's own notes. It waits for you and scores nothing: a streak and a longest streak per range, an option to widen the range after a clean set, and a results strip of speed and misses per note. It feeds the same per-card stats as the stages. Endless runs chain sets of 16. A Pace: Steady beat option (`src/paced.ts`, `docs/changes/paced.md`) keeps the run going at a click, scores each card on the beat (a 200 ms window), offers a tempo from your history and moves it 4 bpm on two clean or rough days; the dock shows beat dots and the count-in lights them. Its misses are reported but do not feed the per-card stats.

**Scales** (`docs/changes/scales2.md`, arpeggios `docs/changes/fingers.md`)
- A Scales tab: Stages (s1 to s13: easy keys, two octaves, D A E and the minors, the black-key keys, all 24 keys, speed, contrary motion, chromatic, triad arpeggios, all 24 triads, dominant and diminished sevenths), Choose a scale (major, three minors, chromatic, arpeggios, sevenths; one hand, together or contrary) and a Quiz (which degree takes the 4th finger).
- Fingering from a fourth-finger rule, shown on the staff as finger numbers, with a Why line; hand and key lines mark fingering still unchecked. MIDI cannot see fingers, so only pitches are checked. Three clean, in-time, even runs in a row make a scale solid.

**Theory and ear** (`docs/changes/ear.md`, `pulse.md`, `chordear.md`, `osk.md`)
- A Theory tab answered on the keyboard in 15-card sessions with Leitner review: chords with inversions and sevenths, key signatures, intervals, cadences, scale degrees, and four ear trainers (play it back, intervals, scale degrees, rhythm echo with bias and spread reported apart). No claim is made about transfer to playing.
- Pulse trainers (`src/pulse.ts`, `pulse-play.ts`): Keep the beat (1, 2 or 4 silent bars; drift, bias and spread kept apart), Subdivisions (eighths, triplets, sixteenths, mixed with swing) and Hold a rhythm against the beat, tapped on any key against a click that always sounds. Numbers in plain words, no points.
- Ear: chords (arpeggiated then blocked, five levels, played back or just the root) and Ear: question and answer (a two-bar phrase, then two bars of your own, judged on form only: 4 notes or more, in the key, last note on the tonic or dominant, a held pulse) (`src/ear2.ts`).
- On-screen keys (`src/osk.ts`, `src/app/osk-ui.ts`): a strip under Theory and Quiz cards and open reading drills and Sight Run with 2 to 4 octaves, chords by several fingers, glides, octave buttons and Names; on when no MIDI keyboard is connected, switchable and remembered. It sends the same message as a keyboard, flagged virtual, so touch evenness is not scored on it.

**Technique and touch** (`docs/changes/touch.md`)
- Technique: 31 exercises in syllabus order (five-finger, scales, hands together, contrary motion, arpeggios, dynamics, minor keys, legato and staccato graded by hold, evenness, melody over accompaniment, rhythm levels 1 to 3), mastery per exercise.
- Touch: key velocity capture, histograms by register, hand balance, session log, personal soft/medium/hard calibration, "Play the file's dynamics", a key-touch setting logged with each run.
- Touch coach (`src/even.ts`, `src/coach.ts`): evenness against neighbours (Technique and Scales, and on songs only a stretch of 8 or more single notes written at one level), live triangles on the staff that fade after three even runs, a quiet test run every fourth run, timing evenness with bias apart from spread, legato and staccato length, voicing between the hands (the hand that carries the melody is found from the notes), and one coaching line.
- Touch on real pieces (`docs/changes/realtouch.md`): MusicXML articulation, slurs and hairpins are read; a song's results say, in at most two lines, whether the written crescendos, accents, staccato and slurs were followed, each from a pattern seen at least three times. No score, no red; off with Touch coach Off, in Read and when waiting.
- Truth pass (`docs/changes/truth.md`): unchecked fingerings say so, the arcade score multiplier is gone, the accuracy HUD shows a dash until a note is judged, and Ready and the review boxes count only clean runs at 90% of the written tempo or more without waiting.

**Practice model** (`docs/STRATEGY.md`, `docs/changes/habit.md`)
- Review scheduling: Leitner boxes (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 days) per Read card, Technique exercise and Theory card (one box climb a day, however many clean passes), spaced review of pieces, a Ready tag after two practised days; due cards dealt more often.
- Today plan of 5, 10, 20, 30 or 45 minutes (`src/plan.ts`): warm-up, scale, reading or Theory, the piece, the worst bars, review. Nothing locks; every row stands alone.
- Effort and skill kept apart: days and minutes only rise; mastery states (New, Learning, Solid, Due) can fall. A day streak with freezes, a break reminder after 30 minutes, a Monday and Tuesday recap with "Most improved", and the Path of five levels (30 requirements) on Progress.
- Fading feedback: judgement words go quiet after eight clean notes in Learn and Technique, note names fade once Read drills show you know them, mastery is graded on unaided runs.
- First week (`src/guide.ts`, `docs/changes/guide.md`): a Start here card on Today with seven suggested days built from real features (Start, Skip, Hide remembered; gone after day 8 and for a player who already has history), a one-line note at the top of Read, Scales, Theory, Technique and Progress the first time, a Connect line on Theory, the Scales Quiz and Sight Run while no keyboard is connected, and a Help sheet (`?` or Settings > Help) that says what each tab is for and what the touch coach cannot see.
- Repertoire ladder (`src/ladder-data.ts`, `src/ladder.ts`, `docs/changes/ladder.md`): 19 named library pieces, 3 or 4 on each Path level, each with Not started, Learning or Ready and a Start that fetches the piece if needed and opens it in Learn.
- Progress: this week, Over time sparklines per kind of practice, Notes you know, reading and technique cells you can tap to practise, Path, touch history.
- The arcade feel is gone: no points, ranks, lives, combos or fireworks; Effects is Off or Calm.

**Songs, library and import**

- Importer: notation layer (`src/quantize.ts`: quantization, chord clusters, tuplets, ties, voices, pickup, onset-weighted key estimate), pure layout (`src/layout.ts`) with rests, ties, beams per meter, tuplets (including irregular), graces, 8va and 15ma, key and time changes, repeats, endings, D.C./D.S./Fine/Coda; MIDI key changes, MusicXML spelling and keys, hands from track names, cues and graces, tempo fallbacks, `scoreFlags` (`docs/changes/importer.md`).
- Report a problem (`src/report.ts`, `docs/changes/report.md`): a quiet link on library start cards and under each piece in Credits opens a prefilled GitHub issue, or a `mailto:` once `REPORT_TO` holds an address; Settings > Data > Practice summary writes a plain-text summary for a teacher.
- Options per imported file (facts, track selection with a hand per track, share, remove); Simplify per song (full, thin chords, melody and bass, melody) with an automatic level for busy arrangements.
- Sounds: sampled grand piano (Salamander, CC BY 3.0) and five lazy-loaded instruments (electric piano, organ, harp, strings, music box; `docs/sounds.md`).

## Research

Findings that still matter. Full reports are in `docs/research/`.

**Evidence rules** (sources: leafo's sightreading.training, a market survey of 16 apps, a learning-science review, a piano-teaching consensus review reading the ABRSM, RCM and Trinity syllabi, and a theory, ear and rhythm survey; strength tags S strong, M moderate, W weak, F folklore):
- Testing and spacing: S for verbal material; MIDI lets the answer be played, which no screen quiz can do. Transfer of ear training to playing: W.
- Fix-it loop (find the bar with the most errors, loop slowly, N clean runs in a row, chain it back): Duke, Simmons and Cash 2009, M/W, consensus.
- Sight-reading: Mishra 2014 meta-analysis (S: small effects, volume helps); no study shows flashcards transfer, so Sight Run should point at real music; leafo's open bug #169 shows the trap of a scroll mode where a late note is a miss.
- Timing and touch: bias reported apart from spread; Skoove ignores durations; fade live marks as accuracy rises, keep end-of-run summaries, add occasional no-feedback test runs (guidance hypothesis: M; optimal frequency not settled, W).
- Habit: streaks with repair or freeze (Silverman and Barasch 2023: M); break reminder after 30 minutes (injury surveys: association only); competence meters as the reward; no leaderboards, no points as the headline (Hanus and Fox 2015: W; Deci 1999: S).
- Not supported, so not built on: more practice minutes alone, learning styles, "more (or less) feedback is always better", slow practice as proven (folklore), sleep locking in a piece, gamification as a sure motivator, music making you smarter.
- Scale tempo targets from the exam boards (ABRSM grade 1 q=60 rising to 100 at grade 4; RCM eighths at q=69 at level 1); technique order from the ABRSM and RCM syllabi: five-finger, C G D F major hands separate, contrary motion, arpeggios, remaining keys, black-key family starting B and D flat (Chopin's order); dynamics steady, then crescendo and decrescendo, then accents; evenness before absolute level.
- Evenness engine design: per note, velocity minus the median of its neighbours (window 5 to 7, same hand, single line, runs of 6 or more, first and last note and octave boundaries ignored, velocity under 15 ignored); spread is MAD x 1.48; bands are a share of the calibrated soft-to-loud range (beginner about plus or minus 12, intermediate 8, advanced 5 of 127, as Touch coach strictness); noteheads marked by deviation (never red); one coaching line after 3 or more repeats of a pattern; a Fixed key touch makes it unmeasurable.

**Library sources, by value** (agent survey; counts verified where noted): Mutopia (788 piano-tagged pieces of 2,124; per-piece PD, CC BY or CC BY-SA; LilyPond, MIDI and PDF but no MusicXML; best teaching coverage: Burgmueller, Czerny, Schumann, Diabelli); PDMX (about 254k MuseScore MusicXML scores, 222,856 after dropping licence conflicts, the only source likely to hold beginner pieces at volume; the licence tag is the uploader's claim, so sample-audit it and filter to piano); OpenScore Lieder (1,352 songs, CC0, proofread; the piano part is accompaniment); Chopin first editions from the Chopin Institute (512 Humdrum files, CC BY 4.0, mostly intermediate and up; 194 of the piano and song files ship); Craig Sapp's Humdrum sets (Beethoven, Mozart, Joplin, Scarlatti, Haydn; licence unclear, ask first); Open Goldberg (CC0, one work); IMSLP (889k PDF scans, per-file licences, no bulk dump; manual gap-filling only). Excluded: anything NonCommercial or NoDerivatives (DCML, MAESTRO, ASAP, much of music21), MIDI performance datasets, scraped MuseScore, Kunst der Fuge. A realistic first-year total is a few thousand pieces, tens of thousands only through PDMX after filtering; a realistic clean total is 1,000 to 2,500 piano pieces. Mutopia's real solo-keyboard count is 638 (the 2,124 includes every instrument).

**Legal rules for ingestion** (research, not legal advice; a lawyer should read the allow and ban lists before launch): ship pieces first published in 1930 or earlier whose composer, arranger and editor all died in 1955 or earlier (the world-safe test; as of 2026 US public domain covers works published in 1930 or earlier); never take fingerings or editorial marks from modern editions (Henle, Baerenreiter and the like), because editorial readings are what is protected; allowed licences CC0, PDM, CC BY, CC BY-SA, MutopiaBSD; banned NC, ND, unknown, all rights reserved; per piece provenance (composer and death year, first publication year, editors, transcriber, source URL and checksum, source licence, attribution, whether modified, ingest date, takedown status); register a DMCA agent and publish a takedown address; a generated Credits and licences page. CC BY-SA does not infect the app, only adaptations of the music data (corrected notes, added fingerings), which we would release under the same licence.

**PDF reading: dropped** (decision 2026-10-27). Optical music recognition was researched and is not good enough to teach from without human review, so the library stays MusicXML and MIDI only.

**Ingestion and hosting plan** (built; kept for the reasoning):
- Format: keep the source as shipped (MIDI, or piano-only .mxl) and derive the Song on the device; a packed Song format saves under 1 KB a piece over brotli (measured: 6.3 KB source, 1.3 KB brotli, 103 KB as Song JSON), so it is not worth an encoder. 5,000 pieces are about 31 MB raw or 6.5 MB brotli. Bundle a base set for the iPad offline; serve the rest from `quartz:/opt/piano/corpus/` (content-addressed `p/<sha>.mid|.mxl`, immutable cache headers, `catalog.json` revalidated, CORS open because the iPad origin is `capacitor://localhost`), cache fetched pieces in IndexedDB.
- Catalogue: `catalog.json` with composers and licences as lookup tables, client-side prefix search over title, composer, opus and tags with diacritics stripped.
- Pipeline quality gates: parse, note and bar counts, pitch range, bar lengths, declared key against estimated key (off by 3 or more goes to review), hand-split sanity, chord and span limits, flags for D.C./D.S., repeats, odd tuplets. Automatic grade from onset rate, notes per bar, chord size, hand span, range, accidentals and rhythm (explainable terms); a hand override wins.
- QA still owed: a perfect-player playthrough through the judge for every piece, layout checks, sampling by ear and eye, Report a problem, an exclude list.
- Milestones: M1 importer fixes, M2 pipeline, M3 catalogue and hosting, M4 first 500 pieces at grades 1 to 3 from Mutopia then a few OpenScore (done, grade 4 added), M5 scale-up (open).
- **OpenScore Lieder (decision 2026-10-27: try it; tried, kept)**: voice plus piano; as piano-only it has no melody. Folding the voice line into the right hand (the importer now writes a doubled note once) passed the playability gate on 4 of the 5 songs we ship (right-hand reach at most 14 semitones for 95% of chords) and failed on Das Wandern, whose baritone line sits two octaves under the piano's right hand (reach 24), which stays piano-only. The gate is a test in `src/library.test.ts` and now also a pipeline gate (`--set lieder`, docs/changes/lieder.md): 300 songs built, 188 folded and 112 piano-only, merged with the 500 Mutopia pieces and published, minus the 36 songs by composers who died between 1931 and 1955, held back until a first-publication year of 1930 or earlier is found (superseded: 2,241 live, 1,299 of them Lieder).
